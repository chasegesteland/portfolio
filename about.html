About

I’m a Nuclear Engineering Ph.D. student at UC Berkeley. My thesis work focuses on enabling the additive manufacturing of tungsten alloys, building on prior research in steels and titanium.

My work spans laser-based processing, microstructural control, mechanical behavior, and irradiation effects in materials for extreme environments.
